Test strips tell you what already happened

Most fish keepers test once a week, if that. Ammonia can go from zero to harmful in two days after one overfed weekend, a dead snail behind the rockwork, or a filter that lost its bacteria during cleaning.

By the time the fish are gasping at the surface, the damage is done. Reefkeep watches the water between your tests, so a bad Saturday doesn't become a lost tank.

Questions people ask first

Does it replace my test kit?

For ammonia, nitrite, pH and temperature, yes. Reefkeep doesn't measure nitrate or hardness, so keep your liquid kit for those monthly checks.

How long does the sensor last?

The sensor tip is rated for 12 months. A replacement tip costs $24, and the app reminds you a month before it's due.

Does it work in saltwater tanks?

Yes. Choose saltwater during setup and the limits switch to reef values, including a tighter pH range.

What if my Wi-Fi drops?

Reefkeep stores 7 days of readings and uploads them when it reconnects. If it's offline for more than an hour, you get a text about that too.
